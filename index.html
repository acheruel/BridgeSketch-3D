Road profile

A crest keeps equal elevations at both ends.

Road section

Right-hand traffic. Cycle bridges may be 3–6 m wide with one 1.5–2 m cycle lane; 0 lanes gives a pedestrian-only deck. Remaining width forms outer shoulders.

Outer shoulder · L / R—
Crossfall

The crown line is the profile grade line (0 = deck centreline, + to the right). Slab, barriers, girders and approaches follow the crossfall; girders are set at their own level under it.

Centre median & line

Centred in the road after side sidewalks. Barrier height 1.10 m; island height 200 mm. At least one lane per direction.

Sidewalks

200 mm raised surface, 280 mm thick with a 1 % crossfall to the road. Left/right looking from bridge start to end.

Bridge railings

Steel railings sit on 450 × 280 mm wheel curbs (road face 35 mm in 280 mm); posts at 3 m on one grid along the bridge and approaches. The bridge railings always continue to the end of the return walls. The architectural railing leans 12° outwards, white-grey.

Approach barriers

Pedestrian bridges continue with a wheel curb and 20C railing, or nothing.

Night lighting

Lights switch on from 20:00 to 06:30. Street lights on 10 m poles default to 30 m; handrail LEDs under the top rail of 20C or SDC railings default to 3 m.

Structural system

Two steel trusses carry the path between them; each one ends with an inclined end post. Simple or continuous spans follow the Continuity option. Footbridges only (cyclists and pedestrians).

Girder type

Section dimensions
Centre spacing—

Overhang: outer girder centre to deck edge. Girder deck: 200, 225 or 250 mm concrete slab + 65 mm asphalt on the roadway, 50 mm haunches over the top flanges. Solid slab thickness uses its own depth.

Depth & continuity

Smooth soffit with 400 mm constant depth over bearings. Single spans deepen at abutments.

Concrete finish
Steel finish & bracing

Plate flanges 500 × 50 mm · webs and stiffeners 14 mm · cross-frames at 8 m or less, deep diaphragms at supports. Steel boxes have two 500 × 50 mm top flanges and inclined side webs.

Pier type

One pier arrangement for all interior supports. Bearings 75 mm; level plinths vary with the deck profile (100 mm minimum).

Columns

Outer column spacing is centre to centre (0 = automatic, 64 % of the deck); inner columns are equally spaced.

Pier cap

Width along the road; thickness vertically. Cap length follows the deck width. Taper start: 0 = outer column face.

Front slope & cones

2H:1V embankments. Front cones can project ahead of the abutment plane; stone facing is limited to the cone segments.

Crossings by span

Clearance moves the crossing elevation; subsequent bridge edits recalculate it.

Terrain

Natural valley: approach embankments. Follows the road profile: a cut valley. Terrain width is measured across the scene (140 m by default; curved approaches may need more). 2H:1V embankments with quarter-cone ends.

Surroundings

Trees stay outside the bridge corridor and the area between crossing roads or railways. Change the seed for another layout.

Sky

Atmosphere computes Rayleigh, Mie and ozone scattering, with a real twilight. Rain and snow add a light fog. Weather and birds are in the view menu, top right.

Rendering

Render quality and season are in the view menu (top right). Glow applies to Medium and High.

Export IFC · BIM model

Every member becomes an IFC element with its class (beam, slab, wall, column, footing, railing, bearing), a name, a material, a colour and a property set (role, span or support, chainage, paint). Concept geometry: the shapes are faceted, not parametric.

BridgeSketch 3D

A quick visual tool for bridge concepts.

By Anthony Chéruel · open source under the MIT License

Starfield and grass species adapted from CK42BB/procedural-stars-threejs and procedural-grass-threejs (MIT); atmosphere after Hillaire 2020 (sebh/UnrealEngineSkyAtmosphere, MIT) and Bruneton 2008 (BSD); Preetham sky after Tw1ddle/Sky-Shader and the three.js Sky object (MIT); river details after Noniv/web-ocean (MIT).

Version 0.7.1

Two bridges, each on its own: « Add bridge B » makes an exact copy of bridge A, then the « Bridge A / Bridge B » switch at the top of the panel chooses the one that the whole panel edits. Works with or without an imported terrain. Bridge B is placed by dragging it on a small plan (round handle to turn it). The terrain import is a two-column workspace: the map on the left, where the bridge and the zones are moved directly (click, Shift + drag to frame several, drag to move, Delete, Ctrl + Z), the settings on the right. The meadow grows on every open ground of an imported site, and the satellite image can be taken off and put back.

Version 0.6.9

Imported sites made faithful and editable: water visible from above, measured ground kept under the bridge (a conflict is reported instead of a dug valley), sun path turned with geographic north (compass in the view). A neutral bridge to set before placing it on the site; water, river, forest, grass and road zones editable here without a new extraction, with a manual water level; road markings and traffic on the imported roads. Bridge B: a second, independent bridge on the same site. Transfers from QuickerAtlas are acknowledged (received, applied), and File › Save complete project keeps bridges, site and zones in one .bs3dproject file.

Version 0.6.8

Import DTM/DSM: pick a block on the satellite map in QuickerAtlas (LiDAR 1 m from NRCan, water, forest and road zones you can edit, the bridge axis) and place the bridge on the real terrain, with its fills, cones and footings on the ground. Truss footbridges: trusses outside the curbs so the approach curbs run straight on, bearings on wider caps and seats, hollow rounded tubes. Complete IFC export: curbs, railings, approaches, markings, street lights and bolts, one clean solid per member.

Version 0.6.7

Medium quality and a stylised gradient sky for every example, without the grey haze; quality and season in the view menu; the same wheel zoom step on every mouse, whatever the frame rate; messages clear themselves within 10 s; adjustable cross-frame spacing for steel girders; 1.5 m abutment footings; no column plinth under box girders; trusses: any height, I-section floor beams, no knee braces on pony trusses; the IFC export opens a free online viewer.

Version 0.6.6

IFC export (File › Export IFC…) in IFC 2x3 for Revit, IFC 4 and IFC 4.3 with IfcBridge; steel truss footbridges (pony or through, Warren, Pratt or Howe); an architectural formliner on the abutments; realistic procedural cars and trucks; weather and birds in the view menu, which now stays open, with the time of day on its own bar; a cyclist on every sidewalk and a faster left lane; plinths without a bottom chamfer; the urban setting shelved. Sketchy can now charge a super jump, fly with a cape and bonk everything in free roam.

Version 0.6.5

A clear view: the 3D view and the parameters use the whole window, the views, camera modes, time of day and display switches gather in one drop-down in the top-right corner, Copy link and Export GLB moved to the File menu and the model facts to the foot of the parameters. A GitHub link in the top bar, falling leaves and rain no longer bunch up in the Plan and Elevation views, the time runs in the Fall river example, and the layout holds on an unfolded Galaxy Z Fold in landscape. And Sketchy, our new mascot, has a secret game.

Version 0.6.4

A physical atmosphere as the default sky (with a real afterglow at dusk), a livelier river, icons on the parameter tabs, a collapsible parameters panel, a lower top bar, span lengths under the Elevation view, High quality with glow and no birds by default, and a new first example: Fall river, five steel spans.

Version 0.6.3

A redrawn launch screen where the bridge itself fills in as the scene loads, the road profile moved to the File menu with plan coordinates X, Y (reference point and bearing at the west axis) for curved alignments, and summer-green aspen foliage.

Version 0.6.2

A road profile table (chainage from the west abutment axis, elevations from a chosen datum) with CSV export, full-size procedural trains, fuller tree crowns with denser woods and shrubs, more natural painted girders, no cast shadows at night, a clean 3D view without its title, a flush abutment side and tapered caps that meet their columns, and a much faster Reveal structure.

Version 0.6.1

New pier shapes (rounded or pointed wall ends, flared hammerheads, inclined portals, V piers), an optional light bloom, realistic example concepts, faster parameter edits, clear-coat vehicles with contact shadows and a realistic paint mix, and gull-like birds. Folded parameter groups with a value summary, a reorganised Site tab, formulas in the numeric fields (for example =2*17.4 or (12+3)/2), a File menu with Open, Save, Undo and Redo (Ctrl+O, Ctrl+S, Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Y), a modified marker, a local autosave with a restore offer, and an option to turn the birds off.

Version 0.6.0

Crowned or uniformly sloped deck, continuous girders by default, NEBT intermediate diaphragms and full-width haunches, girder screens, linear cap tapers, the Samuel-De Champlain architectural railing, a 3 m post grid and railings continued over the return walls, crowned and ditched crossing roads (one-way pairs), open-ground spans, a double truck, a seeded starfield, three grass species, concrete finishes, a high-quality PNG snapshot and a phone layout.

Version 0.5.6

Time runs by default (15 min every 5 s, stop with ❚❚), golden hour before sunset (19:00), 1–4 concrete boxes under one deck, a meandering river in the profile-following terrain, Orbit off by default, Stats at the bottom centre.

Version 0.5.5

New structural systems: prestressed concrete box girders, rigid frames (portique), strutted frames with inclined legs, concrete or steel deck arches and tied arches. MTQ Type 311 barrier and 311 A+B steel rail, 12 m street lights with a single davit arm, autumn scenery with falling leaves, moving cloud shadows, pier water marks and a terrain that can follow the road profile. Faster rendering: Balanced by default, an Auto quality, tree and grass levels of detail, on-demand shadows and a Stats overlay.

Concept visualization only. This tool does not perform structural design or verify code compliance.
